\section{Gaps --- Missing Features or Considerations}
% ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.5em]
  \item \textbf{No email marketing / drip campaigns}: This is a glaring omission. Email nurturing is the highest-ROI channel in real estate (open house follow-ups, price drop alerts, new listing notifications to buyer lists). Must be included.

  \item \textbf{No WhatsApp / SMS marketing}: In MENA and French-speaking African markets (the AR + FR locales suggest this is the target), WhatsApp is the primary communication channel. WhatsApp Business API integration for broadcast lists and automated responses is table stakes.

  \item \textbf{No referral / word-of-mouth system}: Real estate runs on referrals. A simple referral tracking program (past clients referring new ones, with incentives) is missing.

  \item \textbf{No listing syndication}: Pushing listings to external portals (Idealista, SeLoger, Bayut, etc.) is often bundled under marketing. If Biens Management doesn't own this, Marketing should.

  \item \textbf{No QR code generation}: For yard signs, flyers, business cards --- linking physical marketing to digital tracking. Trivial to build, high perceived value.

  \item \textbf{No print material generation}: Brochures, property flyers, window display cards. Agencies still use physical marketing heavily. The Design sub-module should cover this.

  \item \textbf{No review / reputation management}: Google Reviews are a top local SEO signal. Automated review request emails/SMS after transactions close would tie into both SEO and brand trust.

  \item \textbf{No A/B testing framework mentioned}: For ads, landing pages, and email subject lines. Without this, the ``optimization'' promise in Strategy \& Planning is hollow.

  \item \textbf{No lead attribution model}: ``Source tracking'' is mentioned but not fleshed out. Multi-touch attribution is needed (a lead saw a Google Ad, then visited via SEO, then converted on a landing page --- who gets credit?). This is what justifies marketing spend.

  \item \textbf{Budget allocation ``plan'' is vague}: Is this AI-recommended? Manual? Template-based? This needs to be concrete --- ideally AI-suggested based on agency size, location, and listing volume.
\end{itemize}
